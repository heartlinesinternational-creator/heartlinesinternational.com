About us

Two students, one clinic, a hundred mothers at a time.

Heartlines International was founded in 2026 by high school students in San Jose, California, to bring practical health support and real encouragement to mothers who are often overlooked.

Our story

Heartlines International began with a mission trip to the Philippines in February 2026, where our co-founder Jason Ham saw firsthand the work of a free maternity clinic that cares for about 450–500 mothers a year, many of them young and on their own. Iron-deficiency anemia is common and serious in pregnancy, yet mothers often skip medication they don't see as essential, and the emotional weight of new motherhood too often goes unaddressed.

So we set out to build something that lasts: health guides in Tagalog and English, checked by the clinic's medical team, handwritten encouragement from student volunteers, and iron tablets the clinic buys locally, delivered to the clinic cycle after cycle, a hundred mothers at a time.

How a cycle works

  1. The clinic's medical team reviews our health guides before anything is printed.
  2. Student volunteers write a card for every mother and pack the kits.
  3. We ship the kits to the clinic and send funds for iron tablets, which the clinic buys locally.
  4. Clinic staff give each mother her kit during prenatal care, and tell us what to improve for the next cycle.
