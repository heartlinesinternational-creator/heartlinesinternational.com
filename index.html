What one kit holds

Every mother in a cycle receives the same complete kit during her prenatal care.

Iron (ferrous) tablets

Bought locally by the clinic's staff with funds we send. Iron supports the normal growth and development of her baby and helps her feel less tired.

Two health guides

One on taking ferrous tablets, one on mental health in pregnancy and new motherhood. Written in Tagalog and English and checked by the clinic's medical team before printing.

A handwritten card

Written by a student volunteer in the U.S. with a word of encouragement and a verse or prayer for each mother.

Our missionHeartlines International nurtures the health, hope, and future of underserved mothers and children around the world — meeting real community needs through health, education, and mental-health support, so that every mother and child knows they are seen and valued.
