Every mother deserves to feel seen and valued.

Heartlines International sends iron supplements, health guides in mothers' own language, and handwritten cards of encouragement to mothers at a partner maternity clinic in the Philippines.

DonateGet involved

What one kit holds

Each cycle, we prepare kits for about 100 mothers receiving prenatal care.

Iron (ferrous) tablets

Bought locally by the clinic's staff with funds we send, for a mother's prenatal care. Iron supports the normal growth and development of her baby and helps her feel less tired.

Two health guides

A guide to taking ferrous tablets and a mental-health guide for pregnancy and new motherhood, in Tagalog and English, checked by the clinic's medical team before printing.

A handwritten card

Written by student volunteers in the U.S. with a word of encouragement and a verse or prayer for each mother.

Our missionHeartlines International nurtures the health, hope, and future of underserved mothers and children around the world — meeting real community needs through health, education, and mental-health support, so that every mother and child knows they are seen and valued.
